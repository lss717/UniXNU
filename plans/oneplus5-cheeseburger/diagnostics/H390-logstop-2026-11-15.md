# H390 log stop / 日志提前结束

User reports rebooting around 01:03 CST after a 00:53:18 fastboot start. Ring boot=23eecad0, 247 valid slots, retained body 341.3–487.0 s. The approximately 95-second gap is an estimate: fastboot time and kernel timestamp origin differ, and user time is approximate. There is no captured panic. The log does not establish whether the machine stopped or just observation stopped.

用户约 01:03 重启；日志末尾约为启动后 487 秒，比墙钟运行时长短约 95 秒。计时起点与用户时间均有误差。无捕获到的 panic，不能据此判断整机停止。

Source confirms the fourth-cell update follows native_log_flush_hook and h103SlotTick in heartbeatThread. Both can enter synchronous h103FlushNow. If the thread blocks, the cell cannot age its successful-write timestamp and may remain green or white. Consequently fixed color alone is not a reliable storage or system health verdict. A safe correction should evaluate write freshness in a separate thread that does not flush, wait on storage, or print logs; it must not clear the writer busy flag or bypass fLock. Even that cannot detect total scheduler failure.

第四色块刷新发生在同步刷盘之后；若线程阻塞，色块不能更新“过期”状态，可能固定绿或白。后续应由不刷盘、不等待存储、不打印日志的独立线程判断写入新鲜度；不得通过强行清除 busy 或绕过 fLock 解锁。此设计仍无法识别调度器整体停止。

The last lsd sample at 483–485 seconds still includes database seeding wait and restoreInactiveInstalls. Same-cache symbolication with slide 0x28cc000 confirms the exact functions. Ordinal 1 lsd and installd samples are absent; this falsifies the proposed missing-peer explanation for this retained interval. Container cache records continue through 479 s. Frames remain zero.

最后 lsd 样本仍等待数据库初始化；同版本缓存已确认符号。第二 lsd 与 installd 均未找到，因此不能继续将它们当成本轮缺失的执行者。容器缓存更新至 479 秒，桌面帧仍为零。

Do not treat missing installd as a reason to force-start it. Next desktop investigation should identify the seeding task/queue inside the observed lsd and the process producing changing container records, rather than alter IPC semantics or enable GPU. Keep the original ring intact for further analysis.

不要仅因缺少 installd 就强行启动它。桌面主线下一步应找出已观测 lsd 内的初始化任务/队列以及容器记录来源，不改 IPC 语义，不启用 GPU；保留原始环。
