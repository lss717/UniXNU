# H424 stable LaunchServices snapshot and BKS closure

Date: 2026-11-19

## Scope

H424 keeps H423's operation semantics and changes only observation behavior:

- suppress the LaunchServices gate until all first 32 slots have completed;
- require sequence 1 through 32, `active=0`, and nonzero end timestamps;
- print once with `stable=1`;
- call the gate from any late sampler, rather than only while lsd is sampleable;
- add dyld range attribution to the existing NativeMetal signal reporter.

There are no scene, display, IOMFB, IOSurface, accelerator, IPC or filesystem
behavior changes.

## Device result

One `fastboot boot` invocation started runtime `build=H424 boot=69ccb748`.
PowerShell treated fastboot progress on stderr as a terminating native-command
error after `Sending OKAY`; the wrapper therefore did not retain the following
line. The target left fastboot, CDC appeared, and the runtime/capture establish
that the boot completed. No second boot was issued.

The 480-second COM3 capture decoded uptime `336.525530-816.533940 s` and
produced 8,413 frames / 1,310,262 bytes with zero CRC, sequence, boot-switch or
stderr errors.

## Stable LaunchServices gate

The unique gate was emitted at `369.067456 s`:

```text
opens=0 reads=27 writes=0 fsyncs=0 xattrs=106 active=0
slow50=5 slow500=0 slow5000=0 max_ms=103 errors=3
first_ops=133 stable=1
```

Exactly 32 slot rows appeared, with contiguous sequence numbers 1 through 32.
No pending record was required. The H423 print race is therefore closed.

The first 32 operation sequence contains:

- sequences 1-8: `com.apple.system.Security` size/value xattrs;
- sequence 9: 512-byte read;
- sequences 10-14: `com.apple.decmpfs` metadata and an 806-byte read;
- sequence 15: 4,096-byte read, residual 1,080;
- sequence 16: 574-byte read;
- sequence 17: `com.apple.FinderInfo`, result 93;
- sequences 18-21: decmpfs metadata and two 160,631-byte reads at 80 and 82 ms;
- sequences 22-26: another FinderInfo ENOATTR, decmpfs metadata and two
  8,192-byte reads;
- sequence 27: another FinderInfo ENOATTR;
- sequences 28-32: decmpfs metadata and a final 1,276-byte read.

Unlike H423's 1,082-ms tail, H424's slowest operation was 103 ms. The burst had
completed at the sampled snapshot. This reinforces that LaunchServices
filesystem latency is not a stable live blocker.

lsd pid 92 was sampled at `398.418410 s`, then remained alive until its SIGBUS at
`675.869295 s`, after the first UFS OCS timeout. The later exit does not explain
the frame gate more than 300 seconds earlier.

## BKS closes late; first frame still absent

The early BKS focus at `350.405720 s` still had only S/Q events and no responder:

```text
id=0x5b9173 responder=0 response=0x0
```

At `640.647751 s` the request finally completed:

```text
responder=56 response=0x5b91d7
R 0x5b9173 -> responder 56
S/D 0x5b91d7 responder 56 -> SpringBoard 27
```

The request was observed around `time_ns=295.451 s` and the response around
`463.247 s`, about 168 seconds later. More importantly, later display gates
remained zero:

```text
705.151566 s  accel=6/0/628/0 dpy=3/18/0/1/0 s345=0/0/0 val=0 xf=0 fr=0
768.070003 s  accel=6/0/628/0 dpy=3/18/0/1/0 s345=0/0/0 val=0 xf=0 fr=0
```

Thus the BKS wait is a real early delay, but a successful BKS reply is not
sufficient to produce a scene or first frame.

## Renderer failure

`mediaserverd` again reached Metal device and command-queue initialization, then
self-terminated:

| pid | SIGABRT | exit | corpse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 29 | 354.676274 s | 354.677073 s | 1 of 5 |
| 99 | 380.542856 s | 380.756365 s | 2 of 5 |
| 106 | 415.910033 | 415.910862 | 3 of 5 |
| 115 | 506.961731 | 506.962477 | 4 of 5 |

All four have fault PC `0x1d00aaf74`, LR `0x1e1f44fa4`, slide `0x4920000`, and
the same six observed frames. The unslid PC is `0x1cbb8af74`.

The first dyld range implementation is not reliable. It selected implausible
images and offsets, including AudioToolbox for a libsystem-style fault address.
The raw addresses and slide remain valid, but names printed by this probe must
not be used. The plugin also still reports `builder#2 build H420`, so its build
marker must be bumped in the next probe even though the new image lines prove
that the new plugin loaded.

## UFS and later failures

The first UFS OCS timeout occurs at `641.745810 s`, immediately after the BKS reply
at 640.647751 seconds. The remainder of the window contains 18 OCS timeouts,
19 retries, 18 recoveries and 42 user traps. The first display gate at 366.998
seconds predates this fault storm, so UFS is not the initial blocker; it becomes
a strong downstream amplifier after BKS closes.

## Updated ranking

1. Repeated `mediaserverd` SIGABRT after Metal command-queue setup is the
   strongest active renderer failure. All four child processes die at the same
   PC before a content-bearing scene appears.
2. The SpringBoard/launchd/BKS request delays initial progress, but H424 proves
   the eventual reply does not restore first-frame progress.
3. LaunchServices performs a finite metadata/read burst and is not blocked in a
   slow filesystem operation at the stable gate.
4. UFS OCS/recovery begins after the early BKS/display gate and drives a later
   multi-process failure cascade.
5. IOMFB selector 4/5, IOSurface transfer and scanout are still downstream; no
   drawable or submitted frame reaches them.

## Next probe

H424 leaves one useful source change and one source defect:

- keep the stable XNU snapshot unchanged;
- replace the flawed image-range scan with a manually declared Darwin `dladdr`
  ABI, which is present in libSystem but hidden from the iPhoneOS SDK header;
- bump the plugin's own marker to H425;
- record the last successful Metal selector before each abort;
- do not touch NativeMobileDisplay, IOMFB, IOSurface or scanout until a drawable
  reaches the renderer gate.
